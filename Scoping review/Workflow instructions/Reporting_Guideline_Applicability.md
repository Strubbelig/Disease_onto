# Reporting Guideline Applicability

## Design classification

The study is a **systematic cross-sectional evaluation of primary digital ontology artifacts**. Its observational units are unique OWL/RDFS class IRIs and their machine-readable annotations, asserted superclass relations, mappings, and provenance. Publications are used only as ancillary evidence about an ontology and are not included studies.

Consequently:

- it is not a scoping review of literature;
- it is not a systematic review or meta-analysis of studies;
- PRISMA and PRISMA-ScR should not be claimed as governing reporting standards;
- risk of bias across studies and effect-size synthesis are inapplicable;
- “screening,” “deduplication,” and a flow diagram remain valid descriptions of artifact ascertainment, but they should not be branded as PRISMA.

No dedicated EQUATOR reporting guideline was identified for cross-sectional evaluations of ontology artifacts. The manuscript therefore follows the **JAMIA Research and Applications** structure and reports the design transparently using applicable observational-study principles without claiming STROBE compliance.
